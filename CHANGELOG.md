# Changelog

All notable changes to Pastlog are documented here. This project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- Menu-bar clipboard history that keeps 50 to 9,999 clips. Pinned clips are never trimmed.
- Captures every pasteboard flavor: plain and rich text, links, images, files, and colors.
- Multi-word search with All, Pinned, Text, Links, Images, and Files filters.
- Paste formatted (`↩`) or as plain text (`⇧↩`), with an option to make plain text the default.
- Quick paste with `⌘1` to `⌘9` and `⌘0`, plus pinning, editing, deleting, and clip info.
- Global shortcuts you can change: open Pastlog (`⌃⌘V`) and a quick menu at the cursor (`⌃⌥V`).
- Excludes apps you choose and skips concealed or transient clips from password managers.
- 11 themes plus Match System, including City Pop · sviera91.
- Optional Dock icon. Duplicate clips move to the top instead of being saved twice.
- History is stored locally in `~/Library/Application Support/Pastlog`.
